Who is it for?

Who can benefit from DYF?

Students Pursuing Their Degree

Build a clearer understanding of your career options while you are still studying. Explore different career paths, recognise your strengths, and identify the capabilities you can develop to prepare for life after graduation.

Graduates Seeking Career Direction

Make informed decisions about your next step after graduation. Explore career options that match your interests and goals, understand where you need to improve, and identify practical ways to move forward.

Job Seekers Preparing for Opportunities

Understand the skills and capabilities you can offer and the areas you need to strengthen. Assess your current position, explore career possibilities, and plan your preparation for future opportunities.

Individuals Exploring New Career Paths

Consider different ways to build your career, including employment, freelancing, entrepreneurship, or a combination of these. Identify a direction that aligns with your interests, capabilities, and goals.

How it works

Your path to a clearer career direction

  1. Step 1

    Understand Yourself

    Explore your strengths, interests, motivations, and current capabilities. Build a clearer picture of where you stand and what matters to you when making career decisions.

  2. Step 2

    Explore Career Possibilities

    Learn about different ways to build a career. Consider employment, freelancing, entrepreneurship, and hybrid career paths to understand the options available to you.

  3. Step 3

    Identify Your Skill Gaps

    Understand the capabilities required for your intended career direction. Identify your development areas and recognise which skills and capabilities need more attention.

  4. Step 4

    Build Your Capabilities

    Develop essential human capabilities and gain a practical foundation in AI. Learn how these capabilities support your growth, adaptability, and readiness for the changing world of work.

  5. Step 5

    Create Your Career Roadmap

    Receive your personalised DYF Report, outlining your current position, career direction, development areas, and possible next steps, plus a take-home AI Prompt PDF to support your continued learning.

What you walk away with

You'll leave with more than notes

  • Your personalised DYF Report. It outlines your current position, career direction, development areas, and possible next steps: a practical roadmap, not a verdict.
  • A take-home AI Prompt PDF. Ready-to-use prompts to support your continued learning, yours to keep.
  • Clarity on your skill gaps. Know which skills and capabilities need more attention for the career direction you want.
  • Human capabilities and a foundation in AI. Build the adaptability and readiness the changing world of work asks for.
  • Continued support. Access to the Kompetenzen career platform and career consultant support, subject to applicable programme and membership terms.
FAQ

Common questions

What is Design Your Future?

A two-day career discovery and future-readiness programme that helps you understand your strengths, interests and career options. It guides you in identifying the capabilities you need to develop and provides a personalised DYF Report with a practical roadmap for your next steps.

Who is it for?

Students pursuing their degree, graduates seeking career direction, job seekers preparing for opportunities, and individuals exploring new career paths.

How long is the programme?

Two days, with eight sessions and 14 hours in total.

Does it guarantee a job?

No. Design Your Future helps you find your direction and your next step. It doesn't promise jobs, interviews or income.

Does it cover freelancing and entrepreneurship?

Yes. You explore employment, freelancing, entrepreneurship and hybrid career paths, so you can choose a direction that fits your interests, capabilities and goals.

What will I receive at the end?

A personalised DYF Report outlining your current position, career direction, development areas and possible next steps, plus a take-home AI Prompt PDF to support your continued learning.
