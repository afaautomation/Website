KOMPETENZEN FINISHING SCHOOL

The 8-Module Upskill Program
designed to get you hired

Module 01

Communication Mastery

Build articulate, confident English communication skills for corporate environments. Master workplace vocabulary, email writing, and professional conversation.

Module 02

Interview Confidence

Crack any HR or technical interview. Learn how to answer behavioural questions (STAR method), frame your story, and handle unexpected questions with clarity.

Module 03

Resume & LinkedIn

Build a recruiter-friendly ATS resume and a powerful LinkedIn profile that gets you noticed. Learn to showcase your skills, projects, and experience effectively.

Module 04

Aptitude & Reasoning

Ace the quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, and verbal ability sections used in off-campus drives, product companies, and MNC recruitments.

Module 05

Body Language & Personality

First impressions count. Learn professional grooming, confident body language, active listening, and how to carry yourself in group discussions and interviews.

Module 06

Group Discussion & GD Training

Master the art of group discussions. Learn how to initiate, contribute, summarize, and stand out in competitive GD rounds at top companies.

Module 07

Mock Interviews & Feedback

Practice with real company-style mock interviews conducted by industry professionals. Get detailed feedback and iterate until you're ready.

Module 08

Placement Drive & Job Support

Access Kompetenzen's network of 100+ hiring partners. Attend mega placement drives, get company-specific preparation, and receive support until you land your first job.
