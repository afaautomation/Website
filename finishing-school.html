FAQ

Common questions

What is Design Your Future?

A two-day, in-person career programme that helps you understand yourself, see your real career options, and leave with a personal career map and your next step.

Who is it for?

Final-year students, fresh graduates, job seekers and working professionals who want clarity on what to do next.

How long is the programme?

Two days, with eight sessions and 14 hours in total.

Does it guarantee a job?

No. Design Your Future helps you find your direction and your next step. It doesn't promise jobs, interviews or income.

What will I receive at the end?

A personal career report, a take-home AI prompt guide, and a written next step you've committed to.
