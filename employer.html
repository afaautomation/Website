FAQ

Questions from companies

Does it cost anything?

No. Registering, posting jobs and receiving matched candidates is free.

Why does my company need approval?

Candidates trust Kompetenzen because every employer is real. We check each company before its listings appear, and we can hide a company's listings at any time.

Where do applications go?

Candidates apply on Kompetenzen with their profile and resume. These are stored safely with us. Our team screens the applications and shares the matched candidates with you.

Can I add my own application link?

No. Applications always come through Kompetenzen, so candidates are never sent to unknown sites.
