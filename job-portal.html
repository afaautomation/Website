FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Kompetenzen Jobs free for candidates?

Yes. Applying is 100% free, with no payment, no limits and no placement charges. One saved profile works for every job or internship you apply to.

What is the difference between a job and an internship here?

Jobs are full-time, part-time, contract or remote roles for graduates and freshers. Internships are shorter, learn-while-you-work roles for students and freshers. Each has its own listing page and its own profile form.

Do I need experience to apply?

No. Many openings are for freshers and say so on the listing. Experience requirements are shown clearly on every opening.

How do I get alerts for new openings?

Sign up free and complete your profile. We notify candidates whose profile matches a new opening, and you can also register for job alerts on WhatsApp.

Can Kompetenzen help me prepare before I apply?

Yes. Build an ATS-friendly resume with the free Resume Builder, or book a one-to-one career consultation in Kochi or Trivandrum.

I am a company. How do I hire through Kompetenzen?

Email hr@kompetenzen.in or message +91 85908 86009 on WhatsApp. We work with 500+ hiring partners and share matched, interview-ready candidates.
