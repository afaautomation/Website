What we can and can't promise

Last updated: 27 November 2026

We work to keep everything on the Kompetenzen website accurate and up to date. This page explains what our checks cover, and the limits of the information and tools on the website. Please read it with our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

1. What "Verified" means on a listing

Every job and internship on our website carries a Verified badge, because each one is either:

  • posted by the Kompetenzen placement team, or
  • posted by a company that registered on our employer portal and that we checked and approved before any of its listings could go live.

The badge does not mean we guarantee the job, the salary or stipend, or how the employer will behave. The role, pay, location and experience needed come from the employer and can change. The employer makes every hiring decision. Expired listings are taken down automatically after their last day to apply.

2. Never pay for a job

Applying through Kompetenzen is free. We never ask for money to apply, to get an interview or to receive an offer letter, and companies on our portal are not allowed to either.

If anyone asks you for a fee, deposit or payment for a listing on this website, or in Kompetenzen's name, do not pay. Report it to hr@kompetenzen.in or on WhatsApp at +91 85908 86009.

Fees for our own training programmes are separate. They are agreed with you in writing when you enrol, and they are never a condition for applying to a listing.

3. Placements and results

Our training and placement support help you prepare and connect you with employers. They do not guarantee an interview, a job, a particular salary, or a placement within a set time. Figures such as placements made and hiring partners, and the stories of past students, describe past results. Your own results depend on your skills, your effort and the job market.

4. Fit scores and AI tools

Fit scores, keyword match results and the AI suggestions in the Resume Builder are produced automatically. They can be wrong or miss things you know about yourself. They are there to help you and the employer, not to decide for you. Check AI-written text before you use it, because you are responsible for what your resume says.

5. Course and career information

Course details, syllabi, durations, schedules, salary ranges and career paths on the website are general information and can change. Please confirm the current details with us before you enrol. Career consultations give guidance based on what you tell us. The choices you make are your own.

6. Other websites

Listings marked "Official portal" take you to a company's own careers website. We also link to Instagram, WhatsApp and Google. We do not control those websites, and their own terms and privacy policies apply.

7. Errors and availability

We try to keep the website running and its information correct, but it may sometimes be unavailable or contain mistakes. If you spot one, please tell us and we will fix it.
